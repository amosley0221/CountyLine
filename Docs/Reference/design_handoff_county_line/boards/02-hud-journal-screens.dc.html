Callouts

Struck verbs — county verbs (Serve paper, Book, Drive the Ford) render struck-through at 60% with the civilian substitute in the object slot. Teaches the loss without a tutorial card.

Incoming line — now Mrs. Lang relaying calls, in her voice. Same slot, same type; the label changes from DEPOT to her name.

Compass — stays. A cheap pocket compass, Reed's own. Firing strips the job (Ford, jail keys, star, "Sheriff" radio answer, Court St. parking), never navigation. The radio may still crackle; it will not answer "Sheriff."

Grade shifts a hair toward overcast: −30% saturation, no warm key. Same street; less of it belongs to you.

Tabs

Ledger — four columns, entries in Reed's hand with a dated cause, +/− in the margin, running "carried" total. No bar. Each column ends in one sentence in Reed's voice ("Tanner still takes my calls"). Thresholds are never numbers: crossing one writes a clerk's remark under NOTES and a faint pencil tick beside that column. The HUD never shows the four ledger words.

Clerk's typed copy — required Settings toggle. Every Caveat line renders as Courier Prime carbon: Inez Padilla's transcription, same words. Try it in Tweaks.

Map — the 1927 wall map, pencil ticks for visited only.

Cases — reports Reed wrote or refused. Each has a county stamp and a fate.

People — who they answer to (ledger tag), what Reed noticed.

Payroll — the budget as a weapon and a leash.
