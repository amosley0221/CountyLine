# Evidence follow-up and County Book consequences

This extends the standalone jail/Bend Lateral study. The three findings come
from the supplied handoff's first-hour graph: a full sealed bottle, boot prints
above the waterline, and no wet clothing. The dialogue, lead workflow and two
supplement choices are prototype-authored interpretations, not quoted handoff
instructions or a final campaign hearing. None establishes cause of death.

## Play

1. Read the original report at the desk and record field notes at Bend Lateral.
2. Open CASES in the County Book. Each initial note unlocks a matching follow-up:
   check the bottle seal, study the prints, or ask Salazar about the clothing.
3. Choose a lead and revisit that evidence/person. RECORD FOLLOW-UP adds the
   finding; canceling leaves the lead pending and awards nothing. One lead is
   active at a time; switch leads or set one aside without losing completed work.
4. At the desk, submit the original report if it is still a draft. Complete or
   set aside the current lead, then choose ATTACH A SUPPLEMENT or REQUEST FURTHER
   INQUIRY. The preview explains the consequence; CONFIRM FOLLOW-UP files it.
5. Read the Ledger and speak with Pruitt. WRITE THE DATE at the desk saves the
   original carbon, completed observations, lead and supplemental record.

Attaching a supplement preserves the original disposition and gives Pruitt no
new inquiry order. Requesting inquiry records a new assignment for Pruitt and
changes his response, the Courthouse/Street prose and the objective. These are
authored case consequences; Pruitt does not yet conduct an offscreen simulation,
produce new medical evidence or trigger a full hearing/newspaper sequence.

## State and save guarantees

- A prerequisite note is required before pursuing a lead. The matching field
  action is required to record it. Repeated selection/completion cannot farm facts.
- Completed follow-up observations can be included/omitted with other field
  notes when submitting a still-draft original report.
- A supplemental record requires a submitted original and at least one completed
  follow-up. All completed follow-up facts are copied into a frozen supplement.
  Filing ends this study's follow-up choices; its preview warns that it locks.
- The original status, carbon facts, omissions, closing wording and original
  Courthouse delta remain unchanged. No new numeric reputation values are invented.
- State is stored in new tagged fields on the existing Version 1 report. Older
  saves default to no active lead, no follow-up facts and no supplement. The loader
  rejects malformed new enums, unknown/duplicate follow-up facts and inconsistent
  filed snapshots without partially replacing the player's state.
- New progress becomes unsaved through the existing reflected state comparison.
  Tests serialize in memory and never overwrite the player's slot.
